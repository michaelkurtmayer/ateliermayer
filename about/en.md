Michael Kurt Mayer was born in Montréal and raised in the Montérégie region of Québec.

He has practiced in Europe and North America. At [Wulf Architekten](https://www.wulfarchitekten.com/) in Germany, he worked on the [Historische Oberamteistraße Museum](https://www.archpaper.com/2026/08/wulf-architekten-museum-historical-oberamteistrases/) in Reutlingen. He later joined [Atelier Peter Zumthor](https://www.zumthor.org/) in Switzerland, where he contributed to the new buildings for the [Los Angeles County Museum of Art](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/14/arts/design/lacma-peter-zumthor-los-angeles.html) and the [Fondation Beyeler](https://www.archpaper.com/2026/10/peter-zumthor-fondation-beyeler-extension/). He founded Atelier Mayer in Montréal in 2026.

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Architecture from [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/architecture/), where he received the Derek Drummond Award in Architecture and the Wilfred Truman Shaver Scholarship. He is currently a Master of Architecture candidate at [Harvard University](https://www.gsd.harvard.edu/), where he was awarded the Plimpton-Poorvu Design Prize. During his studies, he served as a research assistant to Salmaan Craig and as a teaching assistant to [Xaveer De Geyter](https://xdga.be/) and [Adrien Verschuere](https://bau-kunst.eu/en).

He is a citizen of Canada and Germany, and has passed all of the architectural licensing examinations in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
