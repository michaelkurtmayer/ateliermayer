Michael Kurt Mayer was born in Montréal and raised in the Montérégie region of Québec.

He has practiced in Europe and North America. At Wulf Architekten in Germany, he worked on the Historische Oberamteistraße Museum in Reutlingen. He later joined Atelier Peter Zumthor in Switzerland, where he contributed to the new buildings for the Los Angeles County Museum of Art and the Fondation Beyeler. He founded Atelier Mayer in Montréal in 2026.

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Architecture from McGill University, where he received the Derek Drummond Award in Architecture and the Wilfred Truman Shaver Scholarship. He is currently a Master of Architecture student at Harvard University, where he was awarded the Plimpton-Poorvu Design Prize. During his studies, he served as a research assistant to Salmaan Craig and as a teaching assistant to Xaveer De Geyter and Adrien Verschuere.

He is a citizen of Canada and Germany, and has passed all of the architectural licensing examinations in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
